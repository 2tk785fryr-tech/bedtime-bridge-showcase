Who it's for

You shouldn't have to remember to set an alarm.

Or work out what time it needs to be, or hope you heard it.

📅

Regular schedules

A 9-to-5 most days? It handles the exceptions for you: an early appointment, a flight, a day off or a vacation, so you never set or cancel an alarm by hand.

⟳

Shift workers

Days, swings, nights, rotations: a different start time every day, a different alarm every night. Pick your rotation and it works out each workday.

⏰

Heavy sleepers

Ever slept through an alarm, or stopped it and fallen back asleep? It keeps going until you're up.

How it works

Three parts. Set up once.

1

Your shifts

New in 0.6.0

No work calendar needed. Tell the app when you work, whichever way suits you:

  • My week: the same days and hours each week.
  • A rotation: a ready-made shift pattern or your own, with crew changes and a flex time per shift.
  • Your calendar: the way you already do.
  • An import: your work's schedule from a file or a calendar link, kept in the app.

Each shift type (Days, Swings, Nights) gets its own wake-up:

  • A fixed time: always up at 4:30 for Days.
  • A set time before the shift.
  • Worked back from the drive: get-ready time plus live traffic, so you leave on time.

Getting you up

Built so a sleepy thumb can't end your morning.

⏰

One alarm is enough for most people

Stopping an alarm takes a slide, so it's hard to turn off by accident, and backstops follow if you're not up. Heavy sleeper, or you tend to turn an alarm off and fall back asleep? Add a second or third alarm in Settings.

🔔

Real alarms

They ring through Silent mode and Focus, on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

☾

Your next alarm is the snooze

A live countdown on the Lock Screen. When your next alarm comes before the snooze would end, it is the snooze, so two alarms never ring together.

↻

Follow-up rings

An alarm nobody answers rings again. Silence isn't taken as "I'm up".

›

Stop on purpose

A slide ends one alarm, never two. The last one takes slide and hold, so a half-asleep swipe just snoozes it.

🛏

"Am I up?"

With a bed sensor it knows you stopped the alarm but stayed in bed, and keeps the later alarms.

Home Assistant · Apple Home

Backstops

If you're still in bed as your deadline nears, the house gets louder step by step: a lamp, a push, then speakers and every light. Silent on every normal morning.

Your week, your way.

Coming up is now your week. Change one day without touching the rest.

📅

Tap a day

Change just that day: a day off, different hours, or working from home. + Leave, + Different hours and + Trip sit at the top; on a work trip, your first flight or meeting wakes you.

⏸

Skip until…

No alarms for a few days, and you can undo it any time before then.

✓

Checked before bed

Turned off a workday's alarm? A notification at 8 PM says why. Nothing set for the morning? At bedtime one asks: type a time right in it, or sleep in.

Your evening

Get the sleep you meant to.

  • Wind-down before bed, worked back from your wake-up and your sleep goal.
  • Go-to-bed reminders that get firmer if you're still up.
  • Tomorrow's plan in the evening: when you're up, when to leave, and why.
Your morning

Out the door on time.

  • Leave-by reminders from live traffic (Apple Maps, Google or TomTom), checked again in the morning.
  • How's the traffic? Light, moderate or heavy, in colour, and how it compares with your usual drive.
  • Bad-weather extra time: snow or ice moves your alarms earlier.
  • Sleeping-in mornings: nothing on, no alarm.
